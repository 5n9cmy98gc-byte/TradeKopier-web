Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

TradeKopier's website uses very little tracking on purpose. Here's exactly what's stored on your device and why.

1. What we store

NameTypePurpose
tk_langLocal storage (not a cookie)Remembers your chosen language across pages

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on tradekopier.com.

2. Third-party cookies

When you click "Buy now" you're taken to a secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe may set its own cookies there to process your payment securely and prevent fraud — that happens on Stripe's domain, under Stripe's own cookie and privacy policies, not ours.

3. How to control this

Since we only use local storage for your language preference, clearing your browser's site data for tradekopier.com is enough to reset it — no separate cookie banner is needed. Every modern browser lets you clear local storage from its privacy or site-settings menu.

4. Changes to this policy

If that ever changes — for example if we add analytics in the future — we'll update this page and reflect it here first.
