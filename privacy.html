Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This policy explains what data TradeKopier collects, why, and how you can control it. TradeKopier is a small, independently-run product — we collect the minimum needed to issue and support your license, nothing more.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo send your license key and provide support
License key & statusTo validate your license inside TradeKopier
Machine IDTo tie your license to your device once activated
Payment record (via Stripe)To confirm payment and issue the correct plan

We never see or store your card number — that is handled entirely by Stripe.

2. Who we share data with

We use a small number of third-party processors to run TradeKopier's commercial side:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Supabase — database that stores your license record.
  • Resend — sends the transactional email containing your license key.

We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How long we keep it

We keep your license record for as long as your license is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for accounting and support purposes. You can request earlier deletion at any time (see Section 5).

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access to the database is restricted by row-level security policies — only authenticated TradeKopier staff can view license records.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR in the EU or CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, or to ask us not to process it further. To exercise any of these rights, email licencias@tradekopier.com — we'll respond and act within a reasonable time.

6. Children

TradeKopier is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and, where appropriate, notify active license holders by email.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: licencias@tradekopier.com.
