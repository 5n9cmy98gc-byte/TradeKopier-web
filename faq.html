Frequently asked questions

Everything traders usually ask before switching to TradeKopier.

What is TradeKopier?▾
TradeKopier is an add-on that runs inside NinjaTrader 8. It replicates every entry, exit, and protective order from one leader account to as many follower accounts as you connect, in real time.
How many accounts and connections can I use?▾
There's no limit. One master account, unlimited slave accounts and unlimited broker/platform connections at the same time — all included.
Is it compatible with my prop firm?▾
Technically, TradeKopier works with any account connected to NinjaTrader 8, including Lucid Trading, Apex Trader Funding, Tradeify, Bulenox, Alpha Futures, MyFundedFutures and many more. Important: each prop firm has its own rules on trade copiers and trading multiple accounts, and some restrict or prohibit it. Check your firm's rules before using it — complying with them is your responsibility.
What do I need to install it?▾
Just NinjaTrader 8 already installed. You import TradeKopier as a NinjaScript Add-On (Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On), restart NinjaTrader, and activate it with the email and license key we send you.
How do I get my license?▾
Instantly and automatically. Enter your email in the free download section and we'll email you your license key — no waiting, no manual steps.
Is there any cost or commitment?▾
No. TradeKopier is currently free, with no credit card and no commitment — you can stop using it whenever you like. If we offer paid plans in the future, their terms will be shown clearly before you sign up.
What happens if my connection drops?▾
TradeKopier only replicates what your master account does inside NinjaTrader. If your internet or a broker connection drops, that account stops sending or receiving orders until it reconnects. After reconnecting, check that positions match across all your accounts: a disconnection can leave them out of sync, in which case you must correct them manually.
Do you store my broker passwords?▾
No. Your broker connection lives entirely inside NinjaTrader on your own machine. TradeKopier never sees or stores your broker credentials.
What languages is TradeKopier available in?▾
The software interface is available in Spanish and English, with more languages planned. This website is available in English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, and Italian.
What support do you offer?▾
Real human support, straight from the team that builds TradeKopier, by email or Discord. We reply as soon as we can.
