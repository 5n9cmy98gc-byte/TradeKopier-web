Frequently asked questions

Everything traders usually ask before switching to TradeKopier.

What is TradeKopier?▾
TradeKopier is an add-on that runs inside NinjaTrader 8. It replicates every entry, exit, and protective order from one leader account to as many follower accounts as you connect, in real time.
How many accounts and connections can I use?▾
There's no limit. One leader account, unlimited follower accounts, and unlimited broker/platform connections at the same time — all included in every plan.
Is it compatible with my prop firm?▾
Yes. TradeKopier works with any account connected to NinjaTrader 8, including Lucid Trading, Apex Trader Funding, Tradeify, Bulenox, Alpha Futures, MyFundedFutures, and many more — it's optimized to work without conflicts on any prop firm.
What do I need to install it?▾
Just NinjaTrader 8 already installed. You import TradeKopier as a NinjaScript Add-On (Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On), restart NinjaTrader, and activate it with the email and license key we send you.
How do I get my license after buying?▾
Instantly and automatically. As soon as your payment goes through, we generate your license key and email it to you — no waiting, no manual step on our side.
Can I change plans or cancel?▾
Yes. Each plan is billed for a fixed period (monthly, quarterly, or semi-annual) and simply doesn't renew if you don't buy the next one — there's no long-term lock-in. Write to us if you want to switch plans.
What happens if my connection drops?▾
TradeKopier only replicates what your leader account does inside NinjaTrader. If your internet or a broker connection drops, that account simply stops receiving or sending signals until it reconnects — it doesn't place trades blindly.
Do you store my broker passwords?▾
No. Your broker connection lives entirely inside NinjaTrader on your own machine. TradeKopier never sees or stores your broker credentials.
What languages is TradeKopier available in?▾
The software interface is available in Spanish and English, with more languages planned. This website is available in English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, and Italian.
What support do you offer?▾
Real, human support, 24/7 — straight from the team that builds TradeKopier, by email or Discord.
